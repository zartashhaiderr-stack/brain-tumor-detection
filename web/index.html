02 / Method

From pixels to a decision

Four pieces: the data, the model, how it was trained, and where the decision line sits.

01

Data

The Brain Tumor MRI Dataset from Kaggle: glioma, meningioma and pituitary scans are grouped as tumor, and the rest as no tumor. Duplicate images were removed before splitting so no scan appears in both training and testing.

02

Model

EfficientNetB0 pretrained on ImageNet, compared against a custom CNN and a frozen VGG16 baseline. Exported to ONNX so it runs in the browser.

03

Training

The new head is trained with the base frozen, then the whole network is fine-tuned at a low learning rate. Augmentation and class weights handle the small dataset and the 3 to 1 imbalance.

04

Decision line

The model outputs a tumor score from 0 to 1. The cut-off was chosen on validation data so at least 98% of tumors are caught, because a missed tumor costs far more than a false alarm.
