How it works

Three pieces: the data, the model, and the way the decision line is set.

Data

The Brain Tumor MRI Dataset from Kaggle: glioma, meningioma and pituitary scans are grouped as tumor, and the rest as no tumor. Duplicate images were removed before splitting so no scan appears in both training and testing.

Model

EfficientNetB0 pretrained on ImageNet, first trained with its base frozen, then fine-tuned end to end with a small learning rate. Compared against a custom CNN and a frozen VGG16 baseline. Exported to ONNX so it runs in the browser.

Decision line

The model outputs a tumor score from 0 to 1. The cut-off was chosen on validation data so at least 98% of tumors are caught, because a missed tumor costs far more than a false alarm.
